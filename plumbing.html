Common questions

“Will customers hate talking to an AI?”

It identifies itself as an assistant right away, sounds natural, and most callers still prefer it to voicemail. Listen to the recordings — the proof's right there.

“What about real emergencies?”

Triage is conservative by design. Anything that sounds urgent gets transferred straight to your on-call tech — no AI sits on a real emergency.

“We already have an answering service.”

They take messages. Bullpen books the job onto your calendar — and runs about the same cost.

“What if it messes up?”

It starts on your overflow and after-hours calls only — nothing you're already handling gets touched. You review every recording in the first few weeks, so you see exactly what it says before it earns more of your call volume.
