Common questions

“Will customers hate talking to an AI?”

No — most callers dealing with a leak at 11pm still prefer it to voicemail. It identifies itself as a plumbing assistant right away, sounds natural — a US-trained, plain-English voice — and hands off warmly to a person the moment someone asks. Listen to the recordings — the proof's right there.

“Can it tell the difference between a real plumbing emergency and something that can wait until morning?”

Yes — a burst pipe, no water, sewage backup, or gas smell triggers an immediate transfer to your on-call tech, while anything that isn't urgent gets booked for the next available slot instead of waking anyone up. Triage is conservative by design: the assistant does intake only, never judgment, and if the line's busy on a real emergency, a human calls the customer back within 10 minutes.

“Does it book the job, or just take a message like our current answering service?”

It books the job. A message-taking service hands you a name and number and you start the call over from scratch; Bullpen asks the right questions for a plumbing call and puts the appointment straight onto your calendar while the customer's still on the line.

“We already pay $200/mo for a local answering service — why switch?”

Because that $200 covers message-taking, not booking — and real call volume on a per-minute meter runs closer to $1,170/mo once you're past the first handful of calls. The question isn't the flat fee, it's whether the job gets booked before they call the next plumber on Google.

“Will it work with the way we already run our schedule?”

Yes — Bullpen books directly into Housecall Pro or Jobber, so a booked call shows up on your calendar the same way a call you took yourself would, with no separate inbox to check.

“What if it messes up?”

It only touches calls you're not already answering — it starts on overflow and after-hours calls only, so nothing you're already handling gets changed. You review every recording in the first few weeks, so you see exactly what it says before it earns more of your call volume.
