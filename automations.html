Common questions

“What does an automation actually look like?”

Take the Estimate Chaser: a quote goes unanswered for three days, so the customer gets a friendly text — from your number, in your voice — and your office gets a one-line heads-up. Day seven, one more nudge. Day ten, it's flagged for a human call. Nothing robotic-sounding, nothing your customers haven't seen from businesses they already like.

“Do I need the receptionist first?”

No. Most clients start with the phones because that leak is loudest, but every package stands alone. Plenty of shops with a great office still have estimates dying on the vine.

“What does a project cost?”

$2,000–$10,000 flat depending on scope, quoted after the walkthrough — simple single-workflow builds sit at the low end, multi-system builds at the top. Scale-tier receptionist clients get one automation per quarter included.

“Will it work with my software?”

Housecall Pro, Jobber, and Google Calendar/Sheets today; ServiceTitan is coming soon. If your workflow lives in spreadsheets and text messages, that works too — that's most shops.
